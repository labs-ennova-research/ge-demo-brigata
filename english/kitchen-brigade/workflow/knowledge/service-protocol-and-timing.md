# Service Operational Protocol, Mise en Place, and Station Management

This manual defines how the **Operational Sous-Chef** translates an approved menu into an actionable work plan (timeline) across every station of the kitchen brigade.

---

## 1. Brigade Stations and Operational Responsibilities

Every task in the operational plan must be explicitly assigned to one of the following station tags:

- **[GARDE MANGER — Starters Chef]**: receiving and storing raw items, marinades, cold emulsions, starter portioning, cold garnish preparation.
- **[FIRST COURSES — First Courses Chef]**: preparing vegetable or fish stocks/broths, fresh pasta dough, pre-toasting or portioning rice/pasta, *mantecatura* bases.
- **[RÔTISSEUR — Main Courses Chef]**: aging/trimming proteins (fish filleting, meat butchery), reducing brown stocks/fumets, sous-vide (*CBT*) cooking or à-la-minute searing.
- **[ENTREMETIER — Side Dishes Chef]**: trimming, cutting, and blanching vegetables, preparing vegetable purées/veloutés, regenerating and glazing à la minute during service.
- **[PÂTISSIER — Pastry Chef]**: pastry bases (shortcrusts, custards, bavarois, ganaches), blast chilling, baking table breads/grissini, dessert plating and finishing.
- **[SOMMELIER & FRONT OF HOUSE]**: verifying cellar service temperatures, uncorking/decanting, table setup, segregated handling and delivery of plates for allergic guests.

---

## 2. Mandatory Time Windows (The 3 Service Windows)

Every operational plan must be strictly structured into **3 mandatory time windows**:

### Window 1: `T-24h` (Day before the event — Structural prep & receiving)
- Inspecting incoming goods with Kitchen Stewarding and verifying allergen labels.
- Long-resting preparations: stocks, broths, marinades, rested doughs, pastry creams, and bases requiring overnight cold-room rest (+4°C) or blast chilling.
- Chilling white and sparkling wines in dedicated cellar refrigeration.

### Window 2: `T-4h` (4 hours before guest arrival — Station *Mise en Place*)
- Trimming, cutting, and portioning (weighed into separate *gastronorm* pans) of all ingredients by course.
- **Allergen Zone Setup (Purple Protocol)**: setting up dedicated cutting boards, utensils, and sealed containers for courses destined for guests with allergies/intolerances or vegetarian/vegan diets.
- Approved par-cooking (e.g., blanching vegetables and shocking in ice water, partial sauce reductions).
- 15-minute all-hands briefing with Executive Chef, Sous-Chef, Station Chefs, and Sommelier (`T-1h`).

### Window 3: `SERVICE / AT THE PASS` (From guest seating to dessert service)
Standard pass callout cadence (referenced to guest seating time `T0`):
- **`T0 + 15 min` — Starter Out**: *Garde Manger* plating, first wine pour by the *Sommelier*. As soon as the starter clears the pass, the *First Courses Chef* fires the first course (especially critical for risotto: 16–18 min cooking time).
- **`T0 + 40 min` — First Course Out**: à-la-minute *mantecatura*, plating in warmed bowls. Upon the first-course table-clearing call, *Rôtisseur* and *Entremetier* fire the final cooking of the main course and regenerate the side dish.
- **`T0 + 70 min` — Main Course + Side Dish Out**: synchronized pass exit between *Rôtisseur* and *Entremetier* (or joint plating), paired wine pour.
- **`T0 + 100 min` — Dessert Out**: table crumbing, *Pâtissier* plating, optional dessert wine and *petits fours*.

---

## 3. Pass Safety Protocol for Guests with Allergies ("Sentinel Dishes")

Whenever an event includes at least one guest with an allergy or special dietary requirement:
1. The dish destined for the allergic/special-diet guest must be prepared and plated **first**, on a freshly sanitized work surface, before touching any standard plates for that course.
2. It leaves the pass on a plate of a **distinct color or shape** (or under a dedicated *cloche* / marker) and is hand-delivered exclusively by the Dining Room Manager / Maître d', never placed on a shared tray.
3. This safety reminder must appear explicitly inside the `SERVICE / AT THE PASS` window for every affected course.

---

## 4. Hot-Dish Back-Timing Rules at the Pass

1. Firing start times are calculated **backward** from the pass exit time: `start = pass exit − cooking duration − resting time`.
2. Resting time for fish and meats is brief (2–3 minutes) and concludes right at pass exit: no more than 5 minutes may elapse between end of cooking and service at the pass.
3. Each cooked protein must specify **a single** target core temperature.
4. Example for a baked fish main course going out at `T0 + 70 min` with a 15-minute oven cook: load into the oven around `T0 + 52–55 min`, never earlier.

---

## 5. Document Fidelity Rule

The operational plan and briefing must contain **only** information present in the event input data or in this protocol. Never invent:
- cover numbers, seat numbers, or table numbers (refer to special-diet guests strictly as "vegetarian guest", "guest allergic to crustaceans", etc.);
- specific plate colors, seals, or symbols beyond Section 3's exact phrasing ("distinct color or shape", dedicated *cloche* or marker);
- equipment, chemical rinses, or sanitization procedures not specified in this protocol.
