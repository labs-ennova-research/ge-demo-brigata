# Supplier Price List, Standard Portion Weights, and Prep Yields

This document serves as the official reference for the Kitchen Brigade's Stewarding Office to calculate raw material requirements, prep/trim waste, and wholesale purchase costs.

---

## 1. Standard Net Portion Weights per Guest (Edible Net Weight)

Unless explicitly specified otherwise in the event brief:

| Course | Main Net Portion Weight (per cover) | Secondary Ingredients / Condiments |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | 90 g – 110 g | 20 g dressings / garnishes |
| **First Course (Dry pasta / Rice)** | 85 g | 60 g – 80 g main sauce ingredient + 15 g fats/cheeses |
| **First Course (Fresh pasta / Gnocchi)** | 120 g | 60 g main sauce ingredient |
| **First Course (Velouté / Soup)** | 220 ml (approx. 150 g net vegetables) | 30 g croutons / garnish |
| **Main Course (Meat / Fish)** | 160 g – 180 g (net flesh) | 20 g jus / sauce |
| **Vegetarian Main Course** | 150 g – 170 g | 30 g sauce / accompaniment |
| **Side Dish** | 130 g (net vegetables) | 10 g EVOO / herbs |
| **Dessert** | 110 g – 130 g (finished individual portion) | 15 g sauce / garnish |
| **Wine (if included)** | 0.5 bottles per person (approx. 3 glasses total across the menu) | — |

> **Gross Order Weight Formula**:
> `Gross Weight = Net Weight / (1 - % Waste)`
> *(Example: to obtain 1,000 g net sea bass fillet from whole fish with 45% waste, order `1,000 / 0.55 = 1,818 g` gross).*

## 3. Rule for Off-List Ingredients
If a menu recipe calls for a specific ingredient not listed in the tables above, the Kitchen Steward or Controller must:
1. Map it to the closest product category present in the price list (stating the mapping explicitly).
2. Add a **+10% safety margin on gross quantities** to cover line contingencies or test platings.
