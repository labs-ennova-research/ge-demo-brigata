# Official Customer FAQs and Email Reply Templates (NovaServizi S.r.l.)

**Use only the information and templates below to draft email replies to customers.**

---

## Part 1 · Certified Customer FAQs

### FAQ 1 — Courtesy copies and accounting checks on invoices
- **PDF courtesy copy:** sent by the Administration department within **2 business days** of the request.
- **Billing / user pro-rata checks:** accounting reviews on subscription fees or additional user licenses take **5 business days** from intake. While the review is underway, the invoice payment due dates remain as stated on the document unless otherwise communicated in writing by the Administration department.

### FAQ 2 — Operational Support Hours and 24/7 On-Call Coverage
- **Standard Plan:** coverage Monday through Friday, **08:30 – 18:00** (excluding public holidays).
- **Premium Plan:** includes **24/7** on-call coverage exclusively for total platform outages, subject to providing an active **Contract Code (`NS-XXXX`)** with the Premium option. Without a verified Contract Code, after-hours on-call support cannot be activated and dedicated Premium SLAs cannot be guaranteed.

### FAQ 3 — Activating additional user licenses or new modules
- Additional user licenses activated mid-quarter are billed **pro-rata** starting from the first day of the activation month, in the next available billing cycle.
- Adding more than **10 new user licenses** or activating a new application module requires a formal quote from the Sales department (`commerciale@novaservizi-demo.example`).

### FAQ 4 — Company details or IBAN changes
- Requests to update an IBAN for SEPA Direct Debit (SDD) or change a registered company name must be sent by email to `amministrazione@novaservizi-demo.example`, attaching the form signed by the legal representative at least **10 business days** before the due date of the next invoice.

### FAQ 5 — Contract renewal and notice periods
- All annual and quarterly contracts renew automatically unless formal cancellation notice is submitted at least **60 days** prior to the contract's natural expiration date. No operator may grant reductions or waivers via ordinary email.

## Part 2 · Official Reply Templates

> **General style rules for all drafts:**
> - English language, polite, clear, and professional tone.
> - Maximum email body length: **130 words**.
> - Standard closing signature: `Customer Operations Team — NovaServizi S.r.l.`
> - Never include internal triage email addresses in the reply to the customer unless explicitly provided for in the FAQ (e.g., submitting the IBAN change form).

---

### Template A — Informational Reply / Standard Intake
*(Use for regular sales, administrative, or informational requests within authority)*

```text

<Specific answer based strictly on the official FAQs, stating the applicable standard timeframes>.

<Optional bulleted list of missing information, only if required>.

We remain at your disposal should you need any further assistance.

Best regards,
Customer Operations Team — NovaServizi S.r.l.
```
