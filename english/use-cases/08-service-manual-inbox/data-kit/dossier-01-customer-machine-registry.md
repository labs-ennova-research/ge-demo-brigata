## 2. Contractual Rules on Warranty and Service Plans

- **Warranty expiration calculation:** statutory/contractual warranty lasts **exactly 24 months from the Commissioning Date (SAT)**. Once that period expires, every service intervention and every spare part is billable in full to the customer.
- **Consumables exclusion:** components classified as **Consumable** in the official pricelist (`dossier-03-spare-parts-pricelist-and-terms.md`) and the associated replacement labor are **always billable to the customer**, even during the warranty period, unless a manufacturing defect is certified in writing.
- **Spare parts and maintenance kit discount by Service Plan:**
  - **`Standard`** Plan: **0% discount** on spare parts and maintenance kits; support coverage Mon–Fri `08:30–18:00` (no 24/7 on-call coverage).
  - **`Premium H24`** Plan: **15% discount** on out-of-warranty spare parts, consumables, and scheduled maintenance kits; includes 24/7 technical on-call coverage for total machine breakdowns.
- **Scheduled maintenance thresholds (`Heron-X 400`):** intermediate service at **`1,000 h`**, major service at **`2,500 h`**. Customers are proactively notified when **fewer than 50 hours** remain until the next scheduled maintenance milestone.
