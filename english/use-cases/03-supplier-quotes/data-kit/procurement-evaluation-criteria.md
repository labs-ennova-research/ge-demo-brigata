# Supplier Quote Evaluation Criteria — Procurement Department

Standard procedure for comparative evaluation of raw material quotes (3D printing pellets and compounds).

---

## 1. Evaluation Criteria Weights

Each quote is evaluated across 4 criteria with the following percentage weights (total `100%`):

| Criterion | Weight (%) | Contribution Formula |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Unit Price** | `40%` | `Score × 0.40` |
| **2. Delivery Lead Time** | `30%` | `Score × 0.30` |
| **3. Payment Terms** | `20%` | `Score × 0.20` |
| **4. Warranty** | `10%` | `Score × 0.10` |

**Weighted Total Score Formula (scale from 0 to 5.00):**
$$\text{Weighted Total} = (\text{Price Score} \times 0.40) + (\text{Lead Time Score} \times 0.30) + (\text{Payment Score} \times 0.20) + (\text{Warranty Score} \times 0.10)$$

---

## 2. Scoring Grid ( 1 to 5 )

### Criterion 1 · Unit Price (Weight 40%)
- **5 points:** up to `4.30 €/kg`
- **4 points:** from `4.31 €/kg` to `4.60 €/kg`
- **3 points:** from `4.61 €/kg` to `4.90 €/kg`
- **2 points:** from `4.91 €/kg` to `5.20 €/kg`
- **1 point:** above `5.20 €/kg`

### Criterion 2 · Delivery Lead Time (Weight 30%)
- **5 points:** up to `2 weeks`
- **4 points:** `3 weeks`
- **3 points:** `4 weeks`
- **2 points:** `5 weeks`
- **1 point:** `6 weeks` or more

### Criterion 3 · Payment Terms (Weight 20%)
- **5 points:** `90 days` or more
- **4 points:** `60 days` (including 60 days EOM)
- **3 points:** `30 days` (from invoice date or end of month)
- **2 points:** cash on delivery (COD)
- **1 point:** advance payment

### Criterion 4 · Warranty (Weight 10%)
- **5 points:** `24 months` or more
- **3 points:** `12 months`
- **1 point:** `6 months` or less

---

## 3. Missing Data Rule ("not specified")

- If any required criterion (Price, Delivery Lead Time, Payment Terms, or Warranty) is **`not specified`** in the supplier's quote:
  1. Assign **`0 points`** to that individual criterion (never estimate an intermediate score).
  2. Still calculate the weighted total score across the remaining criteria.
  3. Mandatory: mark the quote as **`INCOMPLETE`** in both the comparison matrix and the final ranking.
