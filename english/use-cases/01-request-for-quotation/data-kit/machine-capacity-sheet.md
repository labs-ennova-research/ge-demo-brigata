# Machine Capacity Sheet — LFAM Department (Large Format Additive Manufacturing)

**Internal document for technical pre-qualification of Requests for Quotation (RFQs).**
All customer requirements must be compared against the limits specified in this sheet.

---

## 1. Build Volume and Dimensional Limits

- **Maximum build volume (single monolithic part):** `4000 × 2000 × 1500 mm` (Length × Width × Height)
- **Minimum printable wall thickness:** `6 mm`
- **Available layer height:** `2 mm` to `5 mm`

> **Binding note:** Segmented or multi-part prints to be bonded/assembled are NOT performed for components exceeding the maximum build volume of `4000 × 2000 × 1500 mm`. Any part with even a single dimension exceeding the limit must be classified as **NOT FEASIBLE**.

---

## 2. Supported Catalog Materials

1. **CaraComp-GF30** — Polypropylene reinforced with 30% glass fiber (general purpose, cold tooling, marine)
2. **CaraCarb-CF40** — Polyamide (PA6) reinforced with 40% carbon fiber (high stiffness, autoclave tooling up to 120 °C, aerospace and motorsport)
3. **CaraBio-LF25** — Biopolymer filled with 25% natural wood fibers (furniture, architecture, exhibition setups)
4. **PETG-GF20** — Recycled PETG with 20% glass fiber (outdoor structures, assembly jigs)
5. **PC-CF20** — Polycarbonate with 20% carbon fiber (structural applications up to 110 °C)

---

## 3. Tolerances and Surface Finish

- **Raw print tolerance (As-Printed):** `± 3.0 mm`
- **Post-print CNC milling tolerance (5-axis):** `± 0.25 mm`
- **Surface roughness after CNC milling:** `Ra 1.6 µm` (suitable for lamination molds after coating/sealing)

---

## 4. Indicative Production Lead Times (Standard Delivery)

| Size Tier | 3D Printing Only | 3D Printing + CNC Milling |
|---|---|---|
| **Small** (up to `1500 × 1000 × 800 mm`) | 2–3 weeks | 4–5 weeks |
| **Medium** (up to `3000 × 1500 × 1000 mm`) | 3–4 weeks | 6–7 weeks |
| **Large** (up to `4000 × 2000 × 1500 mm`) | 5–6 weeks | 7–9 weeks |

- **Maximum batch sizes per single order:** up to `10 parts` for small/medium tiers, up to `3 parts` for the large tier.

---

## 5. Exclusions (What We Do NOT Do)

- We do **NOT** produce transparent or optically clear parts (all fiber-filled materials are opaque, gray, or black).
- We do **NOT** manufacture components certified for food contact (Food Contact) or implantable medical use.
- We do **NOT** 3D print metals (fiber-reinforced thermoplastic polymers only).
- We do **NOT** produce components intended for continuous operating temperatures exceeding `120 °C`.
- We do **NOT** split oversized parts into multiple bonded or bolted blocks.
