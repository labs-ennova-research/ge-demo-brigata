# LFAM Composite Materials Technical Datasheets

Official catalog of materials available for Large Format Additive Manufacturing (LFAM).

---

## 1. CaraComp-GF30 (PP + 30% Glass Fiber)

- **Polymer matrix:** Technical copolymer polypropylene
- **Reinforcement:** 30% short glass fiber
- **Density:** `1.14 g/cm³`
- **Tensile Elastic Modulus (X-axis):** `6,800 MPa`
- **Heat Deflection Temperature (HDT @ 1.8 MPa):** `85 °C`
- **Maximum continuous operating temperature:** `70 °C`
- **Water absorption (24h at 23 °C):** `< 0.05%` (excellent resistance to marine environments and moisture)
- **CNC machinability:** Excellent, low tool wear
- **Typical applications:** Room-temperature vacuum infusion molds (VARTM) in the marine sector, master plugs for hulls and decks, chemical-resistant tanks and components.

---

## 2. CaraCarb-CF40 (PA6 + 40% Carbon Fiber)

- **Polymer matrix:** High-performance Polyamide 6
- **Reinforcement:** 40% high-modulus carbon fiber
- **Density:** `1.32 g/cm³`
- **Tensile Elastic Modulus (X-axis):** `16,500 MPa`
- **Coefficient of Linear Thermal Expansion (CTE):** `1.8 × 10⁻⁵ /K` (high dimensional stability across temperature variations)
- **Heat Deflection Temperature (HDT @ 1.8 MPa):** `145 °C`
- **Maximum continuous operating temperature:** `120 °C`
- **CNC machinability:** Good with PCD (polycrystalline diamond) coated tools
- **Typical applications:** Tooling and molds for oven or autoclave curing up to 120 °C (aerospace and automotive/motorsport sectors), high-stiffness drilling and trimming jigs.

---

## 3. CaraBio-LF25 (Biopolymer + 25% Wood Fiber)

- **Polymer matrix:** High-impact modified technical PLA
- **Reinforcement:** 25% regenerated wood fiber from a certified supply chain
- **Density:** `1.18 g/cm³`
- **Tensile Elastic Modulus (X-axis):** `4,200 MPa`
- **Maximum continuous operating temperature:** `50 °C`
- **Aesthetic appearance:** Warm natural wood-effect finish, easily sanded and painted
- **Usage limits:** Indoor use only; not suitable for high structural loads or direct exposure to sunlight and rain.
- **Typical applications:** Designer furniture, interior architectural installations, trade show booths, museum set designs.
