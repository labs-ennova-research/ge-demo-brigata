# Case Studies and Application References

Collection of projects completed by the LFAM department across its four core sectors.

---

## Case 1 · Marine Sector
**Title:** Female mold for steering console and cabin top of a 42-foot yacht ("Project Tirreno")
- **Sector:** Marine
- **Application:** Cold vacuum infusion lamination mold (VARTM) for a deck component.
- **Material used:** `CaraComp-GF30`
- **Part dimensions:** `3400 × 1750 × 850 mm`
- **Machining:** 3D printing + 5-axis CNC milling with `± 0.25 mm` tolerance.
- **Results summary:** Delivered turnkey in 7 weeks, cutting tooling lead times by 35% compared to a traditional mold milled from a solid block.

---

## Case 2 · Aerospace Sector
**Title:** Trimming and inspection jig for wing fairing panel ("Project Stratowing")
- **Sector:** Aerospace
- **Application:** High dimensional stability support fixture (jig) for CNC trimming operations and metrological inspection.
- **Material used:** `CaraCarb-CF40`
- **Part dimensions:** `2800 × 1200 × 650 mm`
- **Machining:** 3D printing + CNC milling of datum surfaces.
- **Results summary:** Weight reduced by 45% compared to the aluminum equivalent, with thermal stability guaranteed up to 110 °C thanks to carbon fiber's low coefficient of thermal expansion.

## Case 3 · Architecture & Design Sector
**Title:** Modular sculptural seating for executive lobby ("Project Agora")
- **Sector:** Architecture and Design
- **Application:** Batch of 6 large-format monolithic seats for an indoor exhibition space.
- **Material used:** `CaraBio-LF25`
- **Part dimensions:** `2200 × 900 × 750 mm` each
- **Machining:** Variable-layer As-Printed surface finish with light sanding on the seating surfaces.
- **Results summary:** Production of the entire 6-piece batch in 3 weeks using a low-environmental-impact bio-based material.

## Case 4 · Automotive & Motorsport Sector
**Title:** Master model for GT car front hood ("Project Apex")
- **Sector:** Automotive / Motorsport
- **Application:** Master model for laying up carbon prepreg molds with an oven curing cycle at 115 °C.
- **Material used:** `CaraCarb-CF40`
- **Part dimensions:** `2100 × 1850 × 550 mm`
- **Machining:** 3D printing + high-precision CNC milling (`± 0.25 mm`).
- **Results summary:** Completed in 5 weeks overall, withstanding 15 thermal cycles at 115 °C with no measurable deformation.
